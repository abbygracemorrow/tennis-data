    About the data: where it comes from and how every number is computed

    Source and what one row is

    The match file is data/atp_matches.csv: {{rows_raw}} rows and 10 columns of ATP men's tour results from {{first_year}} through {{last_date}}, covering {{players}} players and {{tournaments}} tournaments. The data comes from the ATP Tennis 2000–2023 Daily Pull data set by dissfya on Kaggle; I kept ten of its columns and did not change any values (see the README). One row is one main-draw match, with the tournament, date, series, court, surface, round, number of sets, the two players and the winner. The loser is the other player.

    The order of Player_1 and Player_2 carries no information: Player_1 wins {{p1_wins}} of matches, so no statistic here uses that column.

    Rows dropped, added and missing

    • No rows were dropped.
    • One row was added from data/corrections.csv: the 2019 US Open final (Nadal beat Medvedev on 8 September 2019), which the file leaves out. Without it Nadal would have one fewer title. Delete that file and re-run the script to see the effect.
    • Wimbledon was cancelled in 2020 and the {{last_year}} US Open starts after the file ends, so {{slam_editions}} Slam tournaments have data and {{slam_titles}} finals are counted.
    • A full Slam draw has 127 matches. The file has {{slam_min_draw}} to {{slam_max_draw}} per tournament, {{slam_missing}} fewer than {{slam_expected}} in total ({{slam_missing_pct}}). The file does not say why; walkovers are the likely cause. Win rates and streaks are therefore "as recorded".

    Definitions

    • Grand Slam match: a row where Series is "Grand Slam" (Australian Open, French Open/Roland Garros, Wimbledon, US Open).
    • Title: the winner of a Grand Slam row where Round is "The Final".
    • Career Grand Slam: at least one title at each of the four Slams; the completion date is the date of the final that gave the fourth different Slam.
    • Win rate: wins ÷ matches played (wins plus losses) in the group being measured.
    • Percentage points: the difference between two percentages, such as 88.5% minus 81.3%.

    Thresholds, streaks and the globe

    • Best-of-five: players need at least {{min_bo5}} matches ({{n_q5}} qualify). Best-of-three leaders: at least {{min_bo3}} ({{n_q3}} qualify). Gap chart: at least {{min_bo5}} best-of-five and {{min_bo3_gap}} best-of-three ({{gap_n}} qualify).
    • Streak: Grand Slam matches won in a row in date order, across tournaments; a loss resets it. Round order breaks ties on the same date.
    • Champion countries: a hand-typed table, one row per champion, plus approximate marker positions. It is not in the match file.
    • Dashboard: the Series names changed in 2009, so the dashboard adds a Tier filter that groups them: International = ATP 250, International Gold = ATP 500, Masters = Masters 1000, Masters Cup = Tour Finals.
